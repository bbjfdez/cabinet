Maintenance & Convenience
To keep the group lightweight and easy to maintain:
- Animated stickers are not allowed
- Stickers are allowed at a certain point, try to get them fully compressed (3 to 8kb)
- Photos are allowed. Videos at a certain size
- Avoid sending single-emoji messages

For the backup
- Each line must be a new message, a new line can not be a continuation of a same message, you need to manually fix and/or encourage people to avoid line breaks.
- Every member has to have a two-word name. (e.g: "Dyne Larsen", "Peeno Bowen")
- At the date of backup, ensure you are on UTC+1 timezone
- Remove special characters before stripping and pasting data to the Excel spreadsheet

For most recent iteration data of BBJProjeK The Authority, please refer to BBJProjeK The Authority. Group Telemetry.
